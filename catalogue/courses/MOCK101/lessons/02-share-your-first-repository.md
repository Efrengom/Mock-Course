## The goal

Create a GitHub repository with a README that describes a project you would like to build.

## What to submit

Send any one of these in the submission step after this lesson:

- **A link** to your repository.
- **A written summary** of your project idea and what you learned.
- **A file**, such as a PDF or a screenshot of your README.

## How it is reviewed

Your instructor reads your submission and either marks it complete or asks for changes. Submitting unlocks the next part of the course right away; review happens separately.
